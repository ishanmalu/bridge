Bridge

One keyboard and mouse for your Mac and your PC. Push the pointer off the edge and keep going.

PC
Copied text

Either way round

The Mac's trackpad drives the PC, and the PC's mouse drives the Mac. Cross through the edge, or double-tap Right Option or Right Ctrl.

The clipboard follows

Text, images and copied files move the moment you switch. Files land in Downloads/Bridge.

Shortcuts still work

Cmd becomes Ctrl on the PC, so Cmd+C copies there too. Ctrl+C from the PC arrives on the Mac as Cmd+C.

Media keys, both ways

Play, pause, skip and volume act on whichever machine you're driving. Brightness stays put.

Grab to take back

Move a machine's own mouse while it's being driven and it takes control straight back.

Wakes your PC

Switch to a sleeping PC and Bridge sends Wake-on-LAN. Full-screen games keep the pointer.

Just your network

No server, no account. The two machines talk directly, encrypted, after a one-time six-digit pairing.

Small and quick

One Rust codebase, around 2.5 MB on each side, in the menu bar or tray and out of the way.

macOS 13+ on Apple silicon · Windows 10 or 11 · same local network
